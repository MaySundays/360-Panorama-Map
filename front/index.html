Features

This web application shall feature the following:

360 degree Panorama - Each key facilities and areas will show you an overview of their complete features in a form of a 360 degree panorama. Created using threejs(?), you can easily swipe each panoramic photos for more enhanced and efficient immersive experience!

Interactive Map - The map is drawn and animated for it to be much easier and fun to interact. Even waypoints are designed and customized enough to be eye catching and, of course easier for you to locate. Upon clicking these waypoints, you will be directed to the building or facility that you want to find!

Animated Tour Guides - Familiarizing each location is much more easier if we have tour guides! These tour guides are also drawn and animated. They are diverse, ranging from students to even teachers, that are willing to welcome you as a part of the CIC community. They are also ready to help you in discovering each facilities' purpose, available schedules, and authorities you can trust and rely on depending on the facility that they are assigned to!
