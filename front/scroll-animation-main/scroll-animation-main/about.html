ABOUT CAMPUS
EXPLORATION

Also known as "Camp Expo", this application is created as a Capstone project, which aims to become a handheld interactive map for students, faculty members, parents, and other members of the CIC community that can be easily accessible and usable anytime and anywhere. It also includes drawn and animated characters which serves as tour guides for a much better and enhanced user experience.

What problems can be solved by this web app?

Difficulty in locating the facilities and not being informed with their purpose and schedules.

Having to locate the maps scattered everywhere around the school, and fire exits in maps being too small for users to find.

What are used in the creation of Camp Expo?

Three.js - used for 3d and web animation

HTML, CSS, and javascript - used for the website's blueprint, visual style and UI

Wampserver and MySQL - used for the website's backend

Photosphere Viewer.js - used in creation of panoramic photos

Blender and Poly.pizza - used for the creation of 3d assets
